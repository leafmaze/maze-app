Maze

A personal video publishing scheduler.

Maze is a small tool that runs on its owner's own Mac. Short videos are listed in a Google Sheet, and at set times each day the tool uploads the next one to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

It is used by one person, is not offered as a service, and has no accounts, no sign-ups and no servers.
