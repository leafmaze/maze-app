Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

The short version

Maze runs entirely on the operator's own computer. It has no servers, no database and no analytics. It collects no information from anyone, because nobody else uses it. Nothing is sold, shared or transmitted anywhere except directly to Google and TikTok in order to perform the uploads the operator asked for.

What is stored, and where

All of the following is stored in files on the operator's own computer. None of it leaves that computer except as described below.

  • Access tokens for the connected Google and TikTok accounts, used to upload on the operator's behalf. TikTok access tokens expire roughly daily and are refreshed automatically.
  • The video queue — titles, descriptions, tags and links — which lives in the operator's own Google Sheet.
  • Video files, temporarily, while an upload is in progress. They are deleted once the upload finishes.
  • A local log of what was published and when, for troubleshooting.

What is not collected

  • No personal information about any other person.
  • No TikTok or YouTube viewer, follower or audience data.
  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No cookies, beyond the session the operator's own browser uses to view the local dashboard at localhost.

Who the data is shared with

Nobody. Data is transmitted only to the APIs required to do the work the operator requested — Google (YouTube, Sheets and Drive) and TikTok — and only content and metadata the operator entered themselves. There are no other recipients, no third-party processors and no sale or disclosure of data to anyone.

TikTok data

Maze requests a single TikTok permission, video.publish, which allows it to upload videos to the connected account. It does not read posts, profile details, followers, messages or analytics. The only TikTok information retained is the access token, the refresh token and the account's display name, all held locally so uploads can continue without signing in again.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator disconnects the account in Maze, revokes access in the platform's own settings, or deletes the local token file. Either action ends Maze's access immediately and permanently. Temporary video files are deleted after each upload. The queue can be cleared at any time by editing or deleting the Google Sheet.

Security

Tokens are stored in files readable only by the operator's own user account on their own machine. The dashboard listens only on localhost and is not reachable from the internet.

Children

Maze is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than its operator.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page reflects the current version.
